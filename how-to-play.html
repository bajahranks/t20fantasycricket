01

Your route to the top.

Start with a league, build your squad, then follow every point.

01

Join a league

The first screen you will see after logging in is the leagues screen. Here you can join existing leagues.

02

Know your team

Once you have joined a league, the team screen gives you everything at a glance.

  1. Team statsPlayers, total value, points, and current rank
  2. Team playersYour selected XI and each player's details
  3. LeaderboardA direct path to the current standings

Tap a player to view their current-season performance or remove them from your team.

03

Build in the market

Use the market to find players, understand their value, and shape your dream team.

Filter

  • Search by player name
  • Sort by name, price, or points
  • Filter by player type and team

Select

  • Browse available players
  • Tap to add to your team
  • Long press for player stats

Review

  • Track squad size and budget
  • Monitor remaining transfers
  • Save your completed team

Match lock: The save button is disabled after the tournament starts, based on the game situation.

Manage your profile

Four transfers

Between rounds, you can make up to four transfers to optimize your team.

Changing values

Player values change between rounds based on performance. If your team value exceeds the maximum, you will not earn points until it is resolved.
